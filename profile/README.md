## Deckproof

A read-only check for AI-built websites: security, EU legal information and quality, graded A to F.

Sites built with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor or Replit go live in an afternoon. What ships with them is rarely looked at: a database anyone can read, a missing imprint, a tracker firing before consent, a page no screen reader can follow. Deckproof requests only what any visitor already receives, and says what it finds in plain words.

### Repositories

**checks** - the 44 checks we explain publicly, as machine-readable data, with the standard or paragraph each one points at. English and German, CC BY 4.0.

### What the results are, and what they are not

Security and quality findings are technical observations. Items about EU law describe what is technically present and link the rule that applies. They are general information, never an assessment of an individual case, and never legal advice. Keine Rechtsberatung.
